About the AI Safety Council

An independent, global professional body setting the standard for the safe and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence in Occupational Health, Safety, Environment and Risk Management.

The AI Safety Council exists to ensure that the professionals deploying AI across safety-critical industries are competent, accountable and trusted.

As AI becomes embedded in occupational health and safety, environmental management and enterprise risk, the need for a common, defensible standard of professional competence has never been greater. The Council develops globally recognized certifications, publishes competency standards and frameworks, and provides secure verification of credentials trusted by employers worldwide.

Our work brings together practitioners, employers, regulators and researchers to define what “good” looks like for AI in safety — and to hold the profession to it.

What We Do

Professional Certification

Rigorous, competency-based certifications that validate the skills required to apply AI safely and responsibly in the workplace.

Standards & Frameworks

Globally developed competency standards and frameworks that define excellence in AI for safety across roles and sectors.

Credential Verification

Secure, instant online verification of every credential we issue — giving employers confidence in the professionals they hire.

Research & Publications

Position statements, white papers and research that keep the profession ahead of emerging risks and best practice.

Ethics & Governance

A Code of Ethics and governance model that hold our members to the highest standards of integrity and accountability.

Corporate Certification

Organization-wide programs that build AI-safety capability across teams and demonstrate assurance to stakeholders.

Our Approach

Every certification is built on a published competency framework, assessed against defensible criteria, and maintained through continuing professional development. We are independent of any single vendor or platform — our loyalty is to safety and to the public interest.

  • Competency-based, not attendance-based certification
  • Independent and vendor-neutral standards
  • Globally portable, employer-verifiable credentials
  • Maintained through continuing professional development
  • Governed by a public Code of Ethics
