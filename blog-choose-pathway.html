Which AI-Safety Certification Is Right for You? A Guide to the Three Pathways

Insight 28 May 20263 min read

The AI Safety Council offers three Professional-level certifications, each aimed at a distinct role in the safe and responsible use of AI. If you are wondering where to start, this short guide maps the pathways to the work you do.

AISP® — Certified AI Safety Professional

For practitioners who implement AI in safety-critical environments. If you put AI-driven tools into operation and are responsible for doing so safely, AISP is the core credential.

AIRP® — Certified AI Risk Management Professional

For those who manage AI risk — controls, assurance and risk programmes. Ideal if your work is about identifying, measuring and mitigating what could go wrong.

AIIP® — Certified AI Incident Investigator Professional

For those who investigate AI-related incidents, failures and near-misses. Ideal for forensic, audit and post-incident roles where understanding what went wrong is the job.

Still not sure?

Many professionals hold more than one credential over a career as their responsibilities grow. If you are unsure which fits, start with the role you spend most of your time in — or talk to our certification team and we will help you find the right starting point.
