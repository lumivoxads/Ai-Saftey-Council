Certified AI Safety Professional (AISP®)

A competency-based certification for practitioners responsible for implementing AI-driven solutions in safety-critical environments.

LevelProfessional
Duration12–16 weeks
FormatOnline · Proctored exam
CPD40 credits

Programme Overview

AISP® validates that a professional can implement, operate and assure AI systems safely within occupational health, safety and risk environments.

The certification covers the full lifecycle of deploying AI in safety-critical settings — from problem framing and data governance through model assurance, human oversight and incident readiness. Candidates demonstrate competency against the AI Safety Competency Framework through applied assessment and a proctored examination.

Placeholder content — full syllabus, assessment criteria and pricing will be finalized with the certification team.

Who it's for

  • Safety, HSE and risk professionals adopting AI tools
  • Engineers and analysts deploying AI in operational settings
  • Consultants advising on safe AI adoption

What you'll be able to do

  • Assess AI use-cases for safety impact and suitability
  • Design human oversight and fail-safe controls
  • Establish monitoring, assurance and incident response
  • Document compliance against recognized standards

Syllabus

Core concepts, terminology, the AI Safety Competency Framework, and the landscape of AI applications across occupational safety and risk. Placeholder module description.
