Frequently Asked Questions

The AI Safety Council is an independent, global professional certification authority that advances the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence in Occupational Health, Safety, Environment and Risk Management. It develops competency-based certifications, publishes standards and frameworks, and provides secure verification of credentials trusted by employers worldwide.
The Council offers three Professional-level certifications: AISP (Certified AI Safety Professional), AIRP (Certified AI Risk Management Professional) and AIIP (Certified AI Incident Investigator Professional).
The certifications are designed for safety, HSE and risk professionals, engineers, analysts and consultants who deploy, govern or investigate AI in operational and safety-critical settings.
Employers and organizations can verify any AI Safety Council certification instantly and securely using the online credential verification tool by entering the credential ID or certificate number.
